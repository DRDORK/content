AEO + competitive positioning

AEO Watchdog

A working weekly engine that watches how AI assistants answer the questions buyers actually ask, finds where the company is missing and why, decides what to do about each gap, produces the fix, publishes it to staging, and checks the next cycle to see whether the answer moved.

  • Measures two different assets: being named in an AI answer and being cited as a source. The system tracks both and never treats one as a proxy for the other.
  • Senses across three layers: Brand Radar for model-level visibility, a broad SERP/AI-Overview proxy for query breadth, and a directional Claude layer for the blind spot the other tools cannot see.
  • Maintains its own question set: a curated core plus a self-expanding, self-pruning long tail. Discovery uses the cheaper data layer, while promotion into the scarce core set stays a human decision.
  • Turns gaps into specific work: each one is classified into an action lane, then grounded against a live inventory of 596 posts, current schema and real URLs before anything is generated.
  • Keeps state: moves live in a persistent backlog where recurrence raises priority. Greenlight moves a job into generation and opens a deduped Linear issue; Dismiss can resurface; Kill is permanent and enforced in discovery.
  • Produces finished artifacts: existing pages are read and updated in place, new pages are created from CMS fields, and non-page moves become the actual draft, brief or specific outreach ask. Generated copy passes the humanizer and brand rules before staging.
  • Closes the loop: the run presents one approval batch, then the following cycle checks cited-pool presence, distinct-answer appearance and whether the specific published move changed the answer.

What stays human: promotion into the core prompt set, final approval, anything involving another person, and disclosed community participation.

Business value: turns a new distribution problem into a measurable acquisition loop. The target is greater cited-pool presence and high-intent discovery, with less lag between finding a visibility gap and acting on it.
Principle: this is an AI operating system for a recurring business problem: sense, decide, act, remember and measure. The useful part is not another dashboard. It is the closed execution and learning loop around it.
Competitive intelligence

Weekly market intelligence

A Monday research system that gathers the evidence before the synthesis step begins.

  • Pulls app-market data, AI/search visibility, news, paid creative and community discussion from separate sources.
  • Looks at creative and positioning alongside the counts.
  • Checks data freshness and re-fetches stale sources automatically.
  • Turns past errors into explicit failure-mode rules, including source semantics and competitor-domain verification.
  • Delivers an executive report on a recurring schedule.
Business value: gives leadership a current view of competitive moves, positioning and market signals without rebuilding the research every week, so strategy and creative decisions start from current evidence.
Principle: replace the weekly “go look at everything” ritual with a research system that remembers what it learned last time.
Growth analytics + scoring

Signup quality and leadership reporting

A recurring scoring and leadership reporting layer for a consumer SaaS business with more than 4 million users and 2,000+ new users every day. This is high-volume, user-level data: thousands of new people moving through the funnel each week, not a small set of enterprise accounts.

  • Scores every weekly signup cohort with a multi-signal model tied to observed conversion bands.
  • Produces activation and winback target lists in the same run, so the analysis becomes an operating input rather than a static report.
  • Feeds a weekly leadership report that keeps several activation definitions separate and labels each one.
  • Uses versioned logic, historical cohorts and anomaly guards so unusual weeks do not rewrite the model by accident.
Business value: makes high-volume signup data useful for growth decisions: who to activate or win back, whether acquisition quality is changing and where leadership should focus.
Principle: at consumer scale, definitions have to survive volume. Automate the recurring calculation, keep edge cases explicit and make the output usable without hand-cleaning every cohort.
Lifecycle + product feedback

Personalized weekly behavior report

A weekly user report built from data that did not exist as a weekly field.

  • Derives weekly behavior from the difference between reliable snapshots.
  • Uses safe reruns, hashed archival identifiers and freshness checks.
  • Spreads delivery across the week while everyone still sees the same completed reporting period.
  • Uses different variants for premium, freemium, zero-week and no-session states.
  • States the assumptions behind modeled behavioral value and deliberately under-claims it.
Business value: makes product value visible to users, supports retention and habit formation and creates natural upgrade or winback moments without adding another engagement loop.
Principle: product, channel, data, privacy and copy have to be designed together because each choice changes the others.

Agent-readable knowledge

OKF bundle + Explorer

A working proof of the agent-readable knowledge bet. The bundle turns company knowledge into plain files an agent can retrieve directly; the Explorer shows what happens when an agent actually tries to use them.

  • Built to Google Cloud's Open Knowledge Format v0.1: one concept per Markdown file, YAML frontmatter, organized into product, concepts, playbooks and comparisons.
  • The bundle contains 39 concepts generated from AEO Watchdog research and published as text/markdown for direct retrieval rather than page scraping.
  • The Explorer embeds all 39 concepts and retrieves across both frontmatter and body using keyword overlap.
  • Retrieved concepts are shown as cards before the answer is generated, so the evidence path is visible.
  • The answer is synthesized from those retrieved concepts only, with no outside model knowledge.
  • An empty or weak retrieval is useful evidence too: it exposes a gap in the bundle immediately.
Business value: makes company knowledge easier for agents to retrieve and gives the team a direct way to find missing or weak knowledge before those gaps become visibility problems.
Principle: a knowledge architecture should be testable from the consumer side. The Explorer turns “an agent can use this” from an assertion into something you can inspect.
Decision science

Research and diagnostic analysis

I also use AI for one-time analysis when the source data and definitions can be checked and the reasoning can be traced.

  • Growth diagnoses separate market demand from distribution changes and funnel effects.

Behavior-change product

Focus Coach

Uses real session context when someone tries to quit early. It asks what is getting in the way, then cuts an oversized task down to a workable next step.

  • Product specification and system prompt, with tone rules built into the behavior.
  • A session debrief loop and seeded user history for the demo.
  • Intervention logic grounded in procrastination research, with a strict no-guilt tone.
Intended business value: improve session completion and retention by making the product more useful at the exact moment a user is about to quit.
Generative audio

Sage: adaptive focus music

A generative focus-music engine that composes in the browser for the exact length of a work session. The music develops with the session and resolves at the end.

  • Composer-directed rules translated into engine behavior.
  • Built to stay out of the way: no vocals, obvious repeats or abrupt events.
  • Working prototype, research brief and an on-device development path.
Intended business value: deepen product differentiation and session value with adaptive focus audio that can run on-device without per-minute streaming cost.
