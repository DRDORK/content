Flagship product

Sage: adaptive focus music

Sage generates adaptive focus music in real time for the exact length of a session. It started as a commercial idea: give premium customers something they cannot get anywhere else, and create a reason to upgrade. The economics shaped the engineering. Every note is generated on the device, so there is no per-minute licensing, no streaming infrastructure and zero marginal cost, which makes the packaging a product decision instead of a cost constraint.

The engine implements real harmonic theory, withholding the home chord until the session resolves, with consonance guards that keep the music ignorable and platform-native audio on iOS, Android and web. It keeps playing through a session that blocks the entire internet, which no streaming service can do.

Commercial intelligence

Signup-quality engine

Freedom gets 10,000 to 20,000 new signups a week. The scoring system ranks each one by conversion likelihood using 16 demographic and behavioral signals and a 118-point model with four observed conversion bands.

The same system then sends personalized communications that help high-potential users get value from the product and move toward purchase. It runs without human intervention, and it focuses marketing spend on the signups most likely to convert.
