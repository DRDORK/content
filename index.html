Content leadership

Behavior-change content at scale

For nearly a decade at Freedom, I have led content and audience work around attention, digital wellbeing and healthier technology habits.

  • Set editorial direction across the blog, newsletter, lifecycle and product copy, App Store content, social, video, partnerships and research-led campaigns.
  • Hired and managed staff, writers, creators, contractors and agencies, while building the briefs, calendar and review process behind the work.

  • Worked with product, design, engineering, customer success and analytics so content supported product use and retention as well as reach.
The best behavior-change content gives the reader a step they can take now. It explains enough of the research to earn trust and never turns a difficult habit into a moral failure.

Editorial quality and optimization

Skills, plugins and custom GPTs

  • Humanize skill and the freedom-humanizer plugin, built from real editing passes and a detailed taxonomy of AI writing failures.

AEO and agent-readable content

AEO Watchdog tracks where Freedom appears in AI answers, diagnoses why competitors earn citations and stages a specific fix against an existing page.

  • Matches gaps to a live inventory of 596 posts, including headings and schema state.

The systems collect the evidence, apply rules learned from earlier mistakes and deliver an artifact the team can use immediately.

Product and creative prototypes

AI used at the moment it can help

I also build product and creative concepts, not only back-office workflows.

  • Freedom Focus Coach uses session context to diagnose friction and shrink a task instead of letting the user quit.
  • Sage generates low-salience focus music in real time for the exact length of a work session. It is mentioned here without linking to internal documentation.
  • A Veo animation skill, AI ad-production workflows and six hackathon concepts spanning content, ASO, landing pages and adaptive audio.
The operating principle: automate repeat work, make product truth and voice reusable, keep the review loop visible and turn corrected mistakes into permanent rules.
